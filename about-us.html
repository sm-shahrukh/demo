Our story

Smart Moves started in 2020 in Karachi with a simple frustration: booking a flight home meant five tabs, three phone calls and a printed itinerary from a photocopy shop. Our founder built the first version of the site over a weekend so his own family could book without the runaround.

Six years later we are a licensed travel agency with a team of forty across Pakistan, the Gulf and the UK. We still handle every complicated itinerary by hand we just made the simple ones instant.

  • Licensed by the Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority and IATA accredited
  • Transparent fares taxes and fees shown before you pay
  • Real humans on WhatsApp and phone, 7 days a week

Why travellers pick us

Booking a flight should not require a phone call. Everything you need is on one page, priced honestly, and backed by a team that picks up when something changes.

From a last-minute Umrah departure to a corporate group of twenty, the same care applies we just move at your pace.

What we stand for

Six commitments that shape every booking we make.

Honest pricing

The fare you see includes taxes, fees and the airline's own surcharges. No service charge added at the last step.

Fast turnaround

Most tickets are issued within minutes. Umrah and visa file processing runs on a tracked timeline you can see any time.

Secure payments

Card, bank transfer and cash options, all handled through PCI-compliant gateways with no card data stored on our side.

People first

Every booking gets a named consultant. You reach the same person before and after you travel.

One account

Flights, hotels, Umrah and visa applications live in a single dashboard, so nothing gets lost in an inbox.

After-sales care

Delay, cancellation or a missed connection message us and we rebook on the spot, no paperwork marathon.
